GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions.

For plan-specific information, schedule a conversation with Tequlia.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. Select any “Schedule” or “Book” button on this website to open our appointment calendar.

Who provides insurance advice?

Tequlia Pickett is the licensed insurance agent. Administrative support handles scheduling and routine office coordination, not insurance recommendations or enrollment advice.

Should I submit personal medical information here?

No. This public website does not collect medical histories, Social Security numbers, or insurance application details. Ask your licensed agent about an appropriate secure channel when needed.

Does booking guarantee coverage or eligibility?

No. A consultation is not an application, an offer of coverage, or a guarantee of eligibility or enrollment.
